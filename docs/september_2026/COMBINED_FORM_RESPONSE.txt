ARGUS: a reproducible scroll-processing workbench, with demonstrated improvements to inference reliability, rendering and paired-output efficiency, plus a curated research collection of CT fields, trained baselines and failure-analysis resources.

We are Ryan Gurganious and Daine Ball, Team Cinder Covenant. ARGUS connects community tools into an inspectable workflow; we credit the original detector, renderer, CT, annotation, registration and architecture authors. The linked evidence index contains the source, reproducible checks, measured results, exact revisions and limitations.

1. Which scroll data we used

The public control uses PHerc0139 w016 (640-by-896), published ink/supervision/validation masks and a pinned ink_9um checkpoint. We investigated numerical failures on PHerc1667 w029, renderer metadata using PHerc0009B geometry, and renderer output using retained PHercParis4 CT. Hecate efficiency was measured on an exposed PHerc0139 control (16-by-256-by-256). Exploratory PHerc1203 work has not established a reading and is not claimed as discovery.

A local file-manifest audit lists 4,399 candidate field containers across six scrolls: 4,398 have expected chunk filenames and one partial is quarantined. Complete-by-filename counts: 832 PHerc1203 256-cubed high-resolution fields, 342 PHerc0268 128-cubed fields, and 3,224 coarse fields from PHerc0800, PHerc1447, PHerc0139 and PHerc0009B. The inventory totals 18,301,672,008 compressed chunk bytes including the partial, and 21,437,087,744 logical voxel entries in complete containers. These are selected community subvolumes, not new scans, unique coverage or independent examples; chunks were not decoded and overlap is not established. PHerc1203's 2.403-micrometer selection supports self-supervised/structural research, not low-resolution unread-scroll evidence. PHerc0009B raw CT was in coarse pretraining, so its later evaluation is not physically unexposed.

2. How this helps researchers read scrolls

ARGUS provides an executable known-data control route: identify scroll/volume, acquire a specified crop and labels, verify the checkpoint, prepare input, run inference, evaluate the declared region and retain evidence. The release includes the interface, diagnostics, identity checks, exposure accounting and regression tests. It is a reference workflow starting from an existing surface volume, not an autonomous whole-scroll reader.

The public Workbench also runs a bounded Hecate control on one exposed PHerc0139 field. A guarded job binds input, preparation, runtime and checkpoint identities, reports progress, and displays 2D/3D outputs whose hashes exactly match the pinned control. Other inputs are refused. This is a provider integration, not a reading or accuracy result.

The broader work curates the CT selections, trains representation-learning baselines, and preserves derived atlas, geometry, exposure and defect analyses. We credit the source CT, registration, architectures and upstream annotations. Four local checkpoints (PHerc1203 and PHerc0268 MAE, small PHerc0268 DINO/iBOT, multiscroll coarse DINO) total 4,125,955,140 bytes. They are not qualified ink detectors or an accuracy breakthrough.

A concrete failure motivated Villa #1897: on a GTX 1660 Ti, FP16 produced non-finite predictions and a 99.8%-zero TIFF while reporting success. The PR fails explicitly on non-finite output and adds checkpoint-aware input warnings with optional strict refusal. Guarded full-precision output exactly matched the corresponding unmodified output. We credit separate upstream precision-selection work (#1890); finite output does not prove absence of ink.

3. What our changes enable

Villa #1901 records effective surface geometry/settings and fixes a writer defect: pyramid-disabled output advertised five nonexistent levels. Corrected metadata lists only the written level; tested pixels and physical scale are unchanged. Regression covers pyramid on/off, with/without --pre, complementing existing malformed-store reader handling.

Hecate PR #1 reuses model features when both 2D/3D outputs are requested, replacing duplicate feature evaluation with one tiled pass and disk-backed accumulation. Weights and outputs are unchanged. This is a reusable provider contribution developed through ARGUS.

ARGUS's runtime-isolation correction prevents one runtime from presenting another runtime's local holdings as its own. Before/after UI evidence and three regression fixtures accompany it.

Research records cover data selection, baselines, exposure, geometry and failures. The PHerc1203 profile atlas has 1,505 native-coordinate sites with automatic profile classes—not dense pixel-to-CT mapping, human labels or verified sheet/ink truth. Geometry includes 21 surfaces/seven scrolls and 17 paired flatten comparisons; outcomes are mixed and outlier-sensitive, and input geometry is not newly unwrapped area. EXP7 records 204 synthetic positives, 24 topology defects, 48 real-reference configurations and 88 planted defects; these are checks, not independent surfaces. None establishes an unread-scroll reading.

4. Evidence and status

Independent rescoring of PHerc0139 reproduces ROC AUC 0.772238 and average precision 0.578875 on 90,367 validation pixels (22,765 positive); validation and supervision masks are disjoint. This known-domain control is not a new detector result or cross-scroll proof.

The renderer correction passed nine integration tests and three focused C++ targets locally; all 12,288 retained Paris4 control pixels are exactly unchanged. The public package includes failure, fix, hashes and commands.

Three alternating fresh-process actual-CLI Hecate pairs reduce median launch-to-exit from 17.8357 to 11.5913 seconds (35.01%). Outputs are bitwise equal across 65,536 2D pixels and 1,048,576 3D voxels; feature evaluations drop 98 to 49. GTX 1660 Ti 6 GiB, FP32, batch one, two CPU threads; no thermal pause, max 68 C, min available RAM 13.93 GiB, min free VRAM 4003 MiB. One small exposed field; includes startup/output writing, excludes preparation/UI transport. Fresh processes do not mean cold OS cache. No extrapolation to whole-scroll throughput, 2D-only inference, 2.4-micrometer model, or accuracy.

On a larger exposed PHerc0139 16-by-512-by-512 field, three more CLI pairs are exactly equal over 262,144 2D pixels and 4,194,304 3D voxels; feature calls drop 450 to 225. Median process time was 163.010s vs 57.328s, but unequal guard cooling pauses (55–99s vs ~22s) confound the 64.83% difference. The smaller pause-free comparison is cleaner. No unread-target inference was run.

Retained scores: PHerc0268 MAE full-population AUC 0.5404; grouped small-DINO probe 0.5641; PHerc0009B coarse-DINO prototype/linear probes 0.4573/0.5123 (raw-CT pretraining exposure). PHerc1203 MAE validation loss 0.5455 is reconstruction, not ink. These modest/negative baselines do not qualify a detector; audit streamed checkpoint hashes but did not load or rerun models.

The corrected archival supplement states historical transfer-score, preparation, source-binding and aggregation limits; it claims neither tie-correct rescoring nor universal causal failure. The public collection page is metadata only. A private 1.25 MB packet (151 source/result/card items; SHA-256 55d0a506eb8f810773ce495083e14fb418035c7597eefd5da5e51c181d79f93a) is not public. The ~18.30 GB CT and 4.13 GB model payloads remain local; no download route is claimed.

ARGUS's September release is public; four portable CI jobs passed on the preceding source revision. Villa #1896 and #1902 are merged; Villa #1897, #1901 and Hecate PR #1 remain open. The inventory does not establish the assets were first created/shared in September. LLM tools assisted under operator direction.

Our claim is a working research instrument, curated multi-scroll selections, four trained representation baselines, derived analysis, and measured reliability, renderer and paired-output efficiency improvements. We do not claim confirmed unread-scroll letters, increased ink accuracy, independent adoption or a measured increase in discovery probability. CT/model payloads are not publicly downloadable.
